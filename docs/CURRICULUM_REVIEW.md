# P3/P4 review — h11 to h12

Reviewed 25 September 2026 against Singapore MOE’s **2021 primary mathematics syllabus, updated October 2025**, P3 pages 35–36 and P4 pages 37–40. [Official syllabus](https://www.moe.gov.sg/api/media/92bff26d-b2b4-4535-b868-b8415c744b91/2021-Primary-Mathematics-Syllabus-P1-to-P6-Updated-October-2025.pdf).

## Findings addressed

| h11 issue | h12 behaviour |
|---|---|
| A single four-level ladder mixed school years; the README overstated comprehensive coverage. | Explicit P3/P4 selection and skill metadata. Content and evidence remain within the selected year. Scope and practical-work limitations are documented. |
| 24-hour time was presented as a P4 bridge. | Included in P3, with seconds and start/end/duration questions. |
| Geometry and data mixed P3 and P4 topics at lower “levels”. | P3: right-angle comparisons, parallel/perpendicular lines, area/perimeter and scaled bar graphs. P4: degree measurement, symmetry, composite figures, nets, tables, line graphs and pie charts. |
| Pie charts, explicit perpendicular/parallel practice, construction and several decimal/fraction variants were missing or thin. | Added original generated practice, actual diagrams and human-reviewed drawing groups. |
| “Value of digit X” could refer to multiple occurrences of the same digit. | The target place is named explicitly. |
| Remainder choices consistently started with the correct answer. | All choice questions use Fisher–Yates shuffling and unique values. Fraction alternatives are canonicalised. |
| Line-graph “biggest rise” could be ambiguous or have no positive rise. | New line-graph tasks read values and compare differences, without those assumptions. |
| Quick answers were required for progression; assisted work was not tracked explicitly by skill. | Progression uses independent evidence without a speed gate. Hints, wrong attempts, manual work and reveals are distinct. |
| Three misses revealed an answer and advanced automatically. | Hana chooses when to view a solution and move on. Wrong answers do not remove earned stars in studio practice. |
| Offline activation deleted all caches on the shared origin and waited for hundreds of artwork requests. | Atomic local core cache, on-demand external artwork and deletion restricted to Hana’s cache prefix. |

## Content map

| Year | Area | Practice supplied |
|---|---|---|
| P3 | Whole numbers | Place value and words-to-numerals through 10 000; comparison and sequences; four-digit addition/subtraction; tables 6–9; up-to-three-digit multiplication/division by one digit; remainders; two-step stories. |
| P3 | Fractions | Equivalence, simplification, comparison of unlike fractions, addition/subtraction with related denominators within one whole. Given denominators stay at or below 12. |
| P3 | Everyday measures | Money in dollars/cents; compound-unit conversion for length, mass and capacity; seconds, 24-hour time and elapsed/start/end time. |
| P3 | Geometry/data | Rectangle area/perimeter, rectilinear perimeter, angle comparison, line relationships, scaled bar graphs and a drawing group for line construction. |
| P4 | Whole numbers | Place value/comparison/sequences through 100 000; rounding; factors/common multiples; multiplication through four digits by one digit or three digits by two digits; four-digit division and remainders. |
| P4 | Fractions/decimals | Mixed/improper conversions, fraction of a set, unlike fraction arithmetic; decimal places/rounding through thousandths; fraction/decimal conversion; operations with decimals; whole-number division to rounded decimal answers. |
| P4 | Geometry | Missing dimensions of squares/rectangles, composite area/perimeter, angle notation and reading a degree scale, rectangle/square properties, symmetry, nets and solid representations. |
| P4 | Data | Missing table entries, line graphs and pie-chart readings using half/quarter sectors. |
| P4 | Construction | Drawing angles, shapes/solids and completing reflection on a grid. Requires paper and a grown-up’s review. |

## Limits to interpret honestly

- A skill label groups related objectives; a successful sampled question does not prove complete coverage or mastery of each objective. These are representative practice families, not an exhaustive textbook or validated examination bank.
- Fractions are entered by choice in this build; number/money/decimal questions support keyboard, keypad and the original offline digit recogniser. Handwriting recognition must be checked by Hana before submission.
- Diagrams with dimensions are labelled when not to scale. Physical centimetre measurements and protractor/ruler construction should be done on paper. Freehand drawings are saved, not automatically graded.
- Pie-chart practice currently uses half/quarter sectors and avoids introducing percentage or angle calculations as requirements. Additional data representations and multi-step transfer questions can broaden future assessment.
- Default first-look sessions sample all automatically marked skills over multiple sittings. Drawing groups are deliberately excluded from automatic diagnostic scoring.
- Previous rewards survive. A fresh h12 skill map starts without inferred mastery because h11 did not record comparable per-skill evidence.
